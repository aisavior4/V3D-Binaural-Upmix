# V3D Listening Test Protocol

## Target Source

Smartphone video audio.

Preferred test sources:
- Person approaching camera
- Person moving away from camera
- Street ambience
- Underpass / vehicle movement
- Amusement park ambience

## Presets

- ZOOM: close / focused / front
- WIDE: wider ambience
- DEEP: farther / darker / more immersive

## Listening Questions

1. Is the difference immediately perceptible?
2. Does the processed version preserve body?
3. Does it feel wider, farther, or simply thinner?
4. Is the center voice damaged?
5. Is the original better than processed?

## Failure Signals

- Thin sound
- Bass/body loss
- Unclear difference
- Artificial phase feeling
- Center instability
